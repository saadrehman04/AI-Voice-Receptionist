# Treatments

All facts in this file are invented demo data for Sunrise Dental Studio.

## Teeth whitening
In-office whitening takes about 90 minutes and lightens teeth by several shades in one visit.
Teeth can feel sensitive for a day or two afterwards. We don't whiten during pregnancy.

## Root canal
A root canal treats an infected tooth nerve. The dentist confirms whether you need one, and the
price, after an exam and X-ray, so book an emergency visit or a checkup first.

## Braces and aligners
The consultation is free: Dr. Patel checks your bite, takes photos and explains clear aligners
and traditional braces, with a written quote to take home.

## Fillings
We use tooth-coloured composite fillings. Most fillings take under an hour with local
anaesthetic; you can eat once the numbness wears off.
