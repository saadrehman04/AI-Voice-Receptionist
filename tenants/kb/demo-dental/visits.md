# Visits

All facts in this file are invented demo data for Sunrise Dental Studio.

## First visit
New patients get a full exam, X-rays and a cleaning in their first checkup. Please arrive 10
minutes early to fill in a short health form.

## Dental anxiety
If you feel nervous at the dentist, tell us when you book: we can schedule a longer slot, explain
each step before we do it, and you can raise a hand to pause at any time.

## Cleaning frequency
Most adults should have a checkup and cleaning every six months.
