# Car care (Karachi Auto Care)

## What to bring
Please bring your car's service book if you have one, and leave valuables at home.

## Waiting area
There is an air-conditioned waiting area with tea and Wi-Fi while you wait.

## Oil grades
We stock 5W-30 and 0W-20 synthetic oil. For diesel engines we use 15W-40 unless the service book
says otherwise.
