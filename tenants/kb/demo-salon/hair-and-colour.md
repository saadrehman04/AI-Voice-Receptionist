# Hair and colour (Noor Beauty Salon)

## Colour aftercare
After hair colour, wait 48 hours before washing your hair. Use a sulphate-free shampoo and avoid
swimming pools for a week so the colour lasts longer.

## Patch test before colour
First-time colour clients need a quick patch test at least 48 hours before the appointment. It
takes five minutes and can be done on any visit.

## Children
We cut children's hair from age 5, accompanied by a parent. The haircut service applies.
